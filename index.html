How partner reviews will work

Client-facing

Client experiences

Verified clients may leave moderated, publicly visible reviews focused on communication, respect, transparency, and their experience.

Advisor-only

Internal quality reviews

Advisors assess responsiveness, reliability, and coordination. Low ratings or serious concerns flag a provider for human review.

Ratings never replace professional vetting. Serious safety or confidentiality concerns have a separate escalation route. Reviews shown in a future release will be clearly distinguished from demo data.
